# Raccoon World visual options: gallery

Pictures for the owner's 3D-or-not decision ([visual decisions](visual-decisions.md)). The written comparison is the Claude doc "Raccoon World: visual options (3D or not)". Screenshots are each game's own Steam store images, shown here for reference only; Raccoon World copies none of them. The in-engine comparison of Stevie is #11.

## A. Real-time 3D, stylised to look painted

**TUNIC** (TUNIC Team, 2022, [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/553420/TUNIC/)): fixed high isometric camera, low-poly world lit in real time.

![TUNIC wide view](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/553420/ss_9c0184ba83022786f74f4a70ebae5c47d2a3c35c.600x338.jpg) ![TUNIC closer, coloured light](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/553420/ss_5fc49d3c5fa34b0d0e6b6a5621f9062bec7ac4d7.600x338.jpg)

**Death's Door** (Acid Nerve, 2021, [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/894020/Deaths_Door/)): isometric 3D, toon-shaded animal characters.

![Death's Door village](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/894020/ss_83e57d823ecb8559cd3c70cef500b33d4b841787.600x338.jpg) ![Death's Door crow and frog](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/894020/ss_5052e94b4c36c5cdd39de4aa11bbc60af55cb3d7.600x338.jpg)

**Hades II** (Supergiant Games, 2025, [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1145350/Hades_II/)): real-time 3D characters on hand-painted environments.

![Hades II gameplay](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1145350/ss_c8d2b18451a2cc4d5b4fdd78ed84a5e64e051eac.600x338.jpg)

**Our Stevie in 3D** (clockgrove/clockgrove#77 spike; realistic, not yet stylised): the Meshy model walking, three headings.

## B. 3D rendered to painted sprites

![Hades house hub](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1145360/ss_8a9f0953e8a014bd3df2789c2835cb787cd3764d.600x338.jpg) ![Hades boss fight](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1145360/ss_c0fed447426b69981cf1721756acf75369801b31.600x338.jpg)

## C. Hand-painted 2D in a 3D-feeling world

**Cult of the Lamb** (Massive Monster, 2022, [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1313140/Cult_of_the_Lamb/)): hand-drawn woodland animals in a top-down 3D-feeling settlement.

![Cult of the Lamb village](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1313140/ss_f4ca3fd0416c3f41c57bc3809bb45f8f20e684ec.600x338.jpg) ![Cult of the Lamb close-up](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1313140/ss_566aecf445ab0448191f1996600fd7ec80d0a0d7.600x338.jpg)

**Don't Starve** (Klei Entertainment, 2013, [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/219740/Dont_Starve/)): flat hand-drawn, bone-animated characters on a 3D ground plane.

![Don't Starve camp](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/219740/ss_09bb6bdce4fa085c2c4a9a8f48ea52d3051b44bc.600x338.jpg) ![Don't Starve night torchlight](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/219740/ss_38d4d1b21050fc4b3978fcf65c909260d3673fb7.600x338.jpg)

**Spiritfarer** (Thunder Lotus, 2020, [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/972660/Spiritfarer_Farewell_Edition/)): side view, cozy hand-drawn animal cast.

![Spiritfarer raccoon shopkeeper](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/972660/ss_13be8ec2a11cf1f012abbbe9d526447cfff6cdea.600x338.jpg)
