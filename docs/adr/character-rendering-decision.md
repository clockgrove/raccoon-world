# Decision record: how Raccoon World draws its characters

**Status:** Proposed. The owner picks one option below.  
**Date:** November 09, 2026  
**Issue:** #11  
**Supersedes:** the draft [character rendering ADR](character-rendering-3d-vs-2d.md) (v0.4, clockgrove/clockgrove#77, moved here by clockgrove/clockgrove#87)  
**Evidence:** [the comparison report](../reports/stevie-character-rendering/README.md)

## Context

The owner decided on 2026-11-09 to settle 3D vs 2D first. Character size work waits on this decision: clockgrove/clockgrove#158 (per-world sprite scale) and #23 (storybook scale) are on hold.

The first comparison (clockgrove/clockgrove#77) was inconclusive, for three reasons:
- its spike scene's bloom washed out the cohesion shot;
- its 2D arm showed Jimothy, not Stevie;
- its GPU step failed in Windows Edge and in WSL Chromium.

This rerun draws Stevie in Clockgrove's product renderer at the pinned commit (`8bf1957`). The renderer's own lights, grade, bloom, camera and quality tiers shade every arm, and the characters come in through its world-content extension point. The 2D arm is cut from Stevie's own comparison strips. Both #77 failures are diagnosed and fixed in the tooling. Real-GPU frame times wait on the maintainer step on the owner's RTX 2060 SUPER after merge (pending).

The owner's criteria are cohesion first, then closeness to the mockup:
- **Cohesion:** one lighting model, one grade and post pass, matched detail density, consistent edges, and one camera and scale.
- **Zoom range** is a deciding factor (2026-11-09).

## Options

### A. Real-time 3D characters

What it takes:
- Generated meshes: Meshy's mesh, reduced to 10K triangles in the owner's ship form.
- A quadruped rig fitted in code (the spike's `fit.ts`), with clips keyframed or procedural.
- The meshes are drawn through the renderer's extension point as lit node-material meshes.

For:
- **Cohesion:** the strongest lighting fit. The scene's lights shade the geometry and nothing is baked.
- **Identity:** exact at every heading and frame, because it is one model.
- **Zoom:** holds to about zoom 17 at 2× DPR with one 2048² texture.
- **Cost of more:**
  - one more animation is a clip of a few kilobytes, shared across characters on the same skeleton;
  - a variant is free as a tint or scale, and about $0.88 as a new shape.
- **Memory** grows with characters only, not with animations × directions.

Against:
- **Look:** the fur lives only in the texture, so it reads smoother than the painted mockup.
- **Edges:** silhouettes alias, because the product renders without MSAA.
- **Animation:** quadruped clips are authored by hand or in code; no provider makes them for the fitted rig.
- **Production cleanup:** retopology, UVs and weights still need it.
- **Licence:** the Meshy Free-plan route's output is **CC BY 4.0**. Shipping it needs the Meshy credit in the game's credits; the alternatives are a paid plan or the Tripo or fal routes (MIT).

### B. 3D rendered to sprites

What it takes: option A's model and clips, baked offline to albedo, normal and depth frames for each heading, and drawn as lit quads.

For:
- **Cohesion:** the same identity and scene lighting as A, through the baked normals.
- **Draw cost:** a 2D draw cost for each character.
- **Re-bakes:** free, and they take seconds.

Against:
- **Zoom:** detail is capped by the bake resolution. At this run's 192 px cells it holds only to zoom 2.1 at 2× DPR.
- **Memory:** holding the close-up costs frames × headings × 3 maps × about 0.44 MB a cell, so memory scales with animations × directions × characters.
- **Edges:** alpha-tested edges turn blocky once upscaled.
- **Licence:** the same as A.

### C. 2D sprites

What it takes: sheets generated by Assetforge from the approved turnaround for each animation and direction. Normal maps are derived from the silhouette.

For:
- **Look:** the most painterly result, closest to the mockup's rendering style.
- **Licence:** MIT output.
- **Draw cost:** the cheapest per frame.

Against:
- **Cohesion:** only partly lit by the scene. The painted shading is baked into the albedo.
- **Directions:** each strip draws one direction. Today's strips draw front-left (idle) and front-right (walk) only; every other heading is mirrored or stood in.
- **Identity:** it drifts between frames and candidates (the QA failures are recorded).
- **Zoom:** holds to zoom 3.0 at 2× DPR at 192 px cells. The close-up needs about 388 px cells: about 116 MB for each animation of 8 frames × 8 directions × 3 maps, uncompressed, for each character.
- **Cost:** every new animation and every new shape is a generation round for each direction, about $0.10 a candidate strip, plus picking.

## Licence rule for whatever ships

Anything that ships needs either MIT-compatible output or the Meshy credit, "3D model generated with Meshy (meshy.ai)", in the game's credits:
- Meshy Free-plan models, and every render or conversion of them, are CC BY 4.0.
- Tripo (paid credits), fal TRELLIS.2 (MIT weights), the GPT-image turnaround and the Assetforge strips, and anything derived from them, are MIT.

## Open capabilities in the pinned renderer

The comparison needed five capabilities the pinned renderer lacks. They are raised as Questions on the PR, and any change is made in clockgrove/clockgrove:
- zoom beyond 2;
- a device pixel ratio above 1;
- a dusk grade;
- drawing the pack's `path` effect;
- the CI step, which sits in a protected path here.

The zoom and pixel-ratio limits matter to this decision: within today's product limits (zoom ≤ 2 at 1×), all three options hold their detail.

## Decision

**Pending: the owner's pick of A, B or C.** The real-GPU frame times from the maintainer step are added on issue #11 when they are posted.

## Consequences

- **Production rendering doesn't change until the pick.** Character art stays on the current placeholder path.
- **If A or B:**
  - the 3D ship-form work (Assetforge 3D, the material model for meshes) moves earlier;
  - the fitted rig and its clips become production code in this repository;
  - the Meshy credit, or a switch to an MIT route, is decided before anything ships.
- **If C:** #2's set needs the missing directions, and the derived normal maps should come from Assetforge's transformation core rather than this comparison's silhouette dome.
- **Either way:** clockgrove/clockgrove#158 and #23 resume with the picked option's scale, and the zoom and pixel-ratio Questions decide how close the yard's camera may go.
