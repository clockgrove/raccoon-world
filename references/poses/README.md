# Raccoon poses

General pose references from Kirk's own photos of the raccoons who visit his porch, for any raccoon's idle and look-around clips. Use them when it isn't known which raccoon is shown. Photos and frames only, never clips, per Kirk's rule.

- `standing-upright.png` (Kirk, 2026-11-10): a raccoon standing fully upright on its hind legs at the porch door, face-on, behind the screen. It isn't known which raccoon this is.
  - **Hind feet:** flat and plantigrade, long-toed, the whole sole on the ground. This is the clearest front view of the hind feet.
  - **Arms:** hanging out from the sides, with the hands open and the long fingers spread.
  - **Body:** upright, with the head level and looking straight ahead, and a pale grey underside and legs.

  It is the reference for a stand-up or look-around idle, alongside the kit sitting up on the roof ridge in `../stevie/gait-reference/roof-ridge-walk-hd.png` (0.0–0.5 s).
