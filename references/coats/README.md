# Raccoon coats

Coat and fur references from Kirk's own footage, for look-dev (painted albedo and fur groom maps, clockgrove/assetforge#82) and the renderer's fur (clockgrove/clockgrove#242). Frames only, never clips, per Kirk's rule.

- `winter-coat-frames.png` (Kirk, 2026-11-10): three frames at 0.8, 2.0 and 4.3 s from his clip of two adults foraging by the grill in winter coat. It isn't known which raccoons they are.
  - **The coat:** long, silver-tipped guard hairs over a dense, pale under-layer, so the bodies look almost round. The cheek ruffs flare out past the mask, and the tails are thick, with bold black and buff rings.
  - **Next to the summer photos** (`../gracie/`, `../stevie/stevie-window-front.png`, `../stevie/gait-reference/adult-fence-walk.png`), where the coat lies flatter and the build is leaner, the winter fur is longer and denser, with a lighter, frosted tip colour and a bigger silhouette.
  - **Motion:** nose-down foraging, with the front paws splayed like hands and patting the paving. This is a reference for sniff and eat clips.
